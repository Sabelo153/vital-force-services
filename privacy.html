Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Who this notice covers

This notice concerns the Vital Force Landscaping website and enquiries about gardening services in Durban. For privacy questions or requests, email sicelo@vitalforceservices.com or call 082 488 8304.

2. Information you provide

This website has no enquiry form, customer account or online payment facility. If you contact us by phone or email, you may provide your name, contact details, suburb, property address and information about the gardening work you need. Providing enquiry details is voluntary, but without relevant contact and service information we may be unable to respond or arrange work.

3. Why information is used

Enquiry information is used to respond to you, discuss or prepare a quote, arrange agreed work and deal with service questions. Processing may be necessary for steps you request before a contract, performing an agreement, legal obligations or legitimate business purposes, where permitted by the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Where consent is relied on, it may be withdrawn without making earlier lawful processing unlawful.

4. Website technology and cookies

The website itself does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or browser-storage features. The hosting platform may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, and may use cookies for security or access controls. These platform functions are separate from the website content.

Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google and may disclose technical request information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy. Calling or emailing opens your chosen telephone or email service, which handles information under its own terms.

5. Providers, retention and security

Enquiries pass through the phone or email providers used to communicate with you. Hosting and communications providers may process information outside South Africa. Applicable POPIA safeguards must be considered where personal information is transferred internationally.

Personal information should be kept only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected or for applicable legal obligations, and protected with reasonable safeguards. Contact us for details about the retention of a particular enquiry or record.

6. Your requests and complaints

Subject to applicable law, you can request access to your personal information, ask for correction or deletion, or object to processing. Contact us using the details above. Identity verification may be needed to protect your information. You can also raise a complaint with South Africa’s Information Regulator.

7. Changes

This notice may be updated when website features or information-handling practices change. The date above identifies this version.
